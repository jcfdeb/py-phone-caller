# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy Open Source community focused on building reliable, life-safety and sovereign communication software.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and technical rigor toward other contributors and operators
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, deployment environments, and operational constraints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code and architecture feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the reliability of emergency alerting users

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as phone numbers, SIP credentials, Nostr private keys, or physical addresses) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub repository maintainer channels (or, for security vulnerabilities, through the private reporting flow documented in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
